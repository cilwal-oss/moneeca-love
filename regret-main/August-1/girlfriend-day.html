01 — How it started

Before we knew what this was

Long before Sydney, before flights and time-zone math, we were just two kids who happened to grow up close enough to keep running into each other. Best friends first — the kind who don't remember one single "first meeting," because there wasn't one, just years of always being around. Somewhere between then and now, one of us got on a plane to study on the other side of the world, two days before the flight when it actually started. Officially, since 8 July 2026.
